FRED® Services Terms of Use FAQ


1) What can I do with FRED data?

You can do a lot of things with FRED data, graphs, maps, software, and mobile apps for your own personal, non-commercial use such as...

  • Make cool graphs and maps and share them with friends
  • Download data into Excel for homework assignments
  • Support your policy stance on Facebook or your blog
  • Make a sweet app using our FRED through our free API

Just be sure to mention FRED as the service from which you retrieved the data and keep any copyright notices that appear with the data on FRED.

If you’re over age 13, you can create a free, personal account to save data series and any graphs and maps you’ve created. For complete information on personal accounts, see the full FRED® Services Terms of Use and our Privacy Notice & Policy.


2) What can’t I do with FRED data?

You can’t take all the data on FRED and claim it’s a unique product or service. Don’t try to pass off FRED or its related services (Excel Add-In, Widget, or mobile apps) as your own product or try to sell them to anyone. Don’t do any data mining, scraping or extraction of FRED data. Don’t remove or change the FRED logo on the chart.

For complete information see the full FRED® Services Terms of Use


3) Why do some series on FRED have a copyright notice?

Series with a copyright notice are owned by third parties and have special restrictions. Before using data with a copyright notice for anything other than your own personal use, you must contact the data owner to obtain permission. Unfortunately, the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis cannot give you such permission.


4) I have questions or comments about using FRED, who should I contact?

We welcome your feedback and suggestions for improving FRED. For comments or questions about the data in the FRED® database, we prefer you send an email to stlsFRED@stls.frb.org.


5) How should I cite or provide attribution to FRED?

Use the suggested citation on the "Cite" tab underneath each FRED graph and map. FRED is also compatible with the Zotero citation tool.

For anything other than your own personal use, refer to the full FRED® Services Terms of Use



Summarized FRED® Services Terms of Use


FRED is a database of more than 800,000 economic data series from over 100 sources covering issues and information relating to banking, business, consumer and producer price indices, employment, population, exchange rates, gross domestic product, interest rates, trade and international transactions, and U.S. financial data. In general, the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis encourages the use of FRED data, and associated materials, to support policymakers, researchers, journalists, teachers, students, businesses, and the general public.

FRED provides data and data services to the public for non-commercial, educational, and personal uses subject to a few prohibitions. Furthermore, other uses (e.g., commercial) are subject to additional restrictions. You are free to access and use FRED at no cost, subject to the full terms and conditions of the service.


How Can I Use the Data?

As long as you don’t engage in a prohibited/restricted use and do adhere to any applicable copyright restrictions, you are free to use FRED for your own non-commercial, educational, and personal uses.


I. Intended and Permissible Use

FRED allows you to do a lot of things for your own personal use without requesting permission from the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, such as:

    • Include FRED graphs and maps in a private presentation
    • Create graphs and maps to teach about the data in a classroom setting
    • Include FRED graphs and maps in your personal blog posts
    • Link directly to FRED graphs and maps on the FRED website
    • Create individual visualizations of FRED data
    • View, download, and print FRED content
    • Create an app using a subset of FRED data through the free API; there are additional terms of use applicable to the FRED API
    • Conduct research, and support your own policy positions with FRED data on social media or a personal blog

For any of the above uses, please cite the source of the data and note that it was accessed via FRED. Also, be sure to include any copyright notices that appear with the data. We recommend using the citation provided in the series notes.


II. Prohibited Use

All use of FRED data—including non-commercial, educational, and personal use—is subject to the following prohibitions.

You may NOT…

    • Suggest the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis or FRED endorses your product, service, opinion, arguments, or conclusions.
    • Take all the data on FRED or related services and claim it is a unique product or service or otherwise provide the essential experience of the FRED website, data, or service.
    • Alter visualizations created on the FRED websites (e.g., graphs and maps), including but not limited to the FRED logo, data source(s), titles of graphs and maps, and axis titles.
    • Engage, or otherwise participate, in the use of any data mining, mirroring, robots, scraping, or similar data-gathering or extraction methods except as expressly allowed by the terms of use applicable to the FRED API.
    • Redistribute any third party’s proprietary content, including any graphs, maps, images, logos, data, or datasets, for commercial use without first obtaining express written permission from the data provider.
    • Use the FRED® Services or FRED® Content in connection with the development or training of any software program or system or machine learning, including, but not limited to, large language models, deep learning, generative artificial intelligence, or any other program or process commonly known as artificial intelligence.

III. Use of Data with Copyright Restrictions

Copyright status can be found directly beneath each graph and map on FRED, and the notes below the graph and map provide more information. If you are unsure if a data series is under copyright, please contact us.

There are three (3) types of data series copyright status you will encounter in FRED:


Copyrighted: Pre-approval required

These series are under copyright by a third party. Without first obtaining the express written permission of the copyright holder, the information may only be, these series may only be used for non-commercial educational or personal use. For any other use, you must obtain permission from the copyright holder and include proper attribution of ownership. Neither the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis nor FRED can provide permission(s) on behalf of copyright holders and will not request such permission(s) on your behalf. Link to these series.


Copyrighted: Citation required

These series are under copyright; but, provided you have not engaged in any prohibited uses, you may use these data series with proper attribution of the source and acknowledgment that you obtained the data from FRED (example, “Source: BLS via FRED”) when displaying or publishing it. We recommend using the citation provided in the series notes. Graphs and maps downloaded from FRED are self-citing and do not require a separate citation. Link to these series.


Public Domain: Citation requested

These series may be under copyright or in the public domain and may be used without permission, provided you do not engage in any prohibited use. When using, please cite the data source and acknowledge that you obtained the data from FRED (example, “Source: BLS via FRED”) when displaying or publishing it. We recommend using the citation provided in the series notes. Graphs and maps downloaded from FRED are self-citing and do not require a separate citation. Quite a few series are in the public domain. Link to these series.


IV. Commercial Use

Series with the following copyright labels—Public Domain: Citation requested and Copyrighted: Citation required—may be used for internal commercial uses and may be displayed in textbooks, newsletters, or reports to clients provided that appropriate attribution is given to FRED as well as the original source of the data.
